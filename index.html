International Tax June 2025

Understanding DTAA Benefits: How India's Tax Treaties Reduce Withholding Tax for Non-Residents

India has entered into Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements (DTAA) with 90+ countries. These treaties significantly reduce withholding tax rates on dividends, interest, royalties, and fees for technical services. Understanding the correct application of DTAA provisions can result in substantial tax savings for non-resident entities receiving income from India.

The key lies in obtaining Tax Residency Certificates (TRC) and providing Form 10F to Indian payers to claim the lower treaty rates under Section 90 of the Income Tax Act.

GST May 2025

GST on Import of Services: Reverse Charge Mechanism Explained

When a business imports services from a foreign supplier, the recipient in India is liable to pay GST under the Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM). This applies even if the foreign supplier is not registered in India. Understanding the applicability, rate of tax, and ITC entitlement is crucial for businesses with international service agreements.
